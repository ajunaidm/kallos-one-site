# Kallos One: How to Use

The same guide that appears in the app under Settings › How to Use Kallos One.

Kallos One needs iOS 18 or iPadOS 18, or macOS 15 Sequoia on a Mac, or later.

Built by a working photographer in Sydney. Ready for Australian law out of the box, and usable anywhere you shoot. NSW is the default governing law, and you can choose any other state or country in Settings › Contract Templates › Governing Law.

## Start here

### Set up your studio

Add your business details, logo and signature once. They appear on every contract and invoice.

1. Open Settings › Studio & Brand Identity and tap Add business profile.
2. Enter your legal name, phone, email, website and ABN or Tax ID (optional), and choose a logo.
3. Turn on Default business. Add more profiles if you work under more than one name.
4. Open Universal Master Signature, enter your name, tap the pad to sign full screen, then tap Save Signature. This goes on the photographer side of every release and client agreement.
5. In Contract Templates, open each section (Model Releases, General Client Agreements, and Weddings & Events) and review its templates. Each section has its own templates and its own default, and editing opens full screen with only Cancel and Done.

**Tips**

- On first launch, Get Started walks you through your business details and master signature. Tap Skip to do it later.
- Turn on Biometric Security to lock the app with Face ID or Touch ID. While it's locked, anything opened from Siri, Spotlight, a notification or the menus waits until you unlock, and open windows close when it locks again (except a contract being signed in Signing mode).
- Choose Light, Dark or Device in Appearance, and the card style: Darkroom (glass on warm paper) or Compact grey (plain cards that fit more on the screen). Kallos One follows your text size, up to the largest Accessibility sizes.
- The Getting started card in Bookings ticks off each step and disappears when you're done. Tap Hide to dismiss it sooner.
- On iPhone, the tab bar shows the five everyday tabs — Bookings, Calendar, Models, Crew and Clients. Contracts, Invoices and Settings are one tap away under the menu button in the top-right of each tab. On a Mac it works the same way, with the menu button at the top-right of each tab (or press ⌘6, ⌘7 or ⌘8). On iPad, Contracts and Invoices are in the sidebar too, and Settings opens as a pop-up window from the gear button at the top right (or press ⌘, with a keyboard).
- Choosing a model, template, business, country, currency or governing law opens a list with a search field. Start typing to find it quickly.
- To use Kallos One in Urdu, open the Settings app › Kallos One › Language and choose اردو. Screens, Help, the privacy policy and terms change; contracts and PDFs stay as you wrote them, and the English privacy policy and terms are the ones that count.

### Show only what you shoot

Choose Models, Clients, Weddings & events or Everything. Parts you don't use move under More, so the screens stay simple.

1. The first time you open this version, Kallos One asks What do you shoot? Everything is ticked, so nothing changes unless you choose.
2. Tick what you shoot: Models, Clients, or Weddings & events. Tap Everything to show it all again.
3. Parts you don't choose move under More: in the menu button at the top right (the gear button on iPad), and under + › More in Bookings. Nothing is deleted or switched off.
4. To change it later, open Settings › What You Shoot.

**Tips**

- Without Models, the Models tab and Quick Release are under More. Without Clients, the Clients tab is under More. Without Weddings & events, Plan an Event is under More and the All / Shoots / Events switch is hidden.
- At the top of Bookings, Needs attention lists everything waiting on you in one place: shoots to wrap up, overdue invoices, galleries due, contacts changed in Contacts, notifications turned off, old shoots to archive and a backup that's due. Tap a line to deal with it, or tap Later on the ones that can wait. The Today card stays on its own above it.
- Settings has its own search: type a word such as logo, backup or Urdu, and it finds the right Settings page and the Help topics about it.

### Trading names and tax

Work under more than one trading name, each with its own logo, and keep tax right: trading names that share a Tax ID are summarised together, different Tax IDs stay apart.

1. Open Settings › Studio & Brand Identity and open a business (or add one).
2. Enter the legal name, the Trading name clients see, the logo, and the Tax ID with its type: ABN, NTN, STRN, VAT, GST and others.
3. When booking a shoot or planning an event, choose the Acting business. Its trading name, logo, legal name and Tax ID go on the contract and the invoices.
4. On an event, change the Acting business on the event page; every day of the event follows.
5. Open Invoices › Money Summary. With more than one tax entity, choose one under Tax entity: the figures, the BAS lines and the CSV export are for that entity only.

**Tips**

- Trading names that share the same Tax ID count together on one BAS or tax summary, under the main legal entity (your default business if it's one of them).
- Businesses with different Tax IDs are always kept apart: their invoices never mix in one summary.
- Spaces don't matter: 12 345 678 901 and 12345678901 are the same ABN.

### Write and format contracts

Edit contract templates in Settings › Contract Templates. A few symbols add headings, bold text and lists; they never print on the PDF.

| You type | On the PDF |
|---|---|
| `# Title` | Large heading (document title) |
| `## Section` | Section heading |
| `### Sub-section` | Small heading |
| `**bold words**` | **Bold** |
| `*italic words*` | *Italic* |
| `- Item` or `* Item` | Bullet point |
| `1. Item` | Numbered list |
| A blank line | New paragraph |
| `{{model_name}}` | Merge tag, filled in when the contract is signed |

Example:

```
# Model Release

This agreement is made on {{signed_date}} between **{{business_name}}** and **{{model_name}}**.

## Usage

- Portfolio and website
- Social media
- *Editorial* publications

1. The Model receives no further payment.
2. This release cannot be withdrawn after publication.
```

**Tips**

- Put a space after `#`, `-`, `*` and `1.` at the start of a line: `## Usage`, not `##Usage`.
- Close every `**` or `*` you open, or the stars print on the PDF.
- Lines without a blank line between them are joined into one paragraph.
- The merge tags for each kind of template are listed at the bottom of the template editor, which also has a Formatting card with an example you can copy. Client agreements use client tags such as `{{client_company}}`, `{{client_fee}}` and `{{client_rights}}`. Event agreements use event tags such as `{{event_schedule}}` and `{{package_price}}`. {{trading_name}} prints your trading name. Urdu event agreements use Urdu tags such as {{client_name_ur}} and {{event_title_ur}}.
- Tap Preview to see the contract as it prints, filled with sample details. On a wide iPad or Mac window, the live preview sits beside the text as you type.
- Deleting a template that bookings or events use asks first; they then use that section's default. Contracts already signed never change. Duplicate opens the copy, and Cancel keeps it, only discarding your edits.

**Governing law**

`{{governing_law}}` becomes the state or country whose law applies to the contract. Model releases and client agreements use the same list. Settings › Contract Templates › Governing Law lists every Australian state and territory, plus Pakistan, Japan, Malaysia, New Zealand, England and Wales, the UAE, Singapore, New York and California, and a general "Any country" choice that applies the law of the country where the session took place. Tap + to add another place, and swipe to make one the default. Each template can use its own choice, and a booking can override it under Governing law on the booking's details card.

## Everyday tasks

### Add a model

Add models by hand or from Contacts, and choose whether they also go into Contacts.

1. Open Models and tap +.
2. New Model: enter their first name, middle name (optional) and last name, email and phone, then choose Kallos One Only or Kallos One + Contacts.
3. Import from Contacts: pick a contact. If they have several numbers, emails or addresses, choose the ones to use. To add several people at once, tick them all and tap Done; each gets their first number, email and address, which you can change on their page.
4. On the model's page, search for their address, add their date of birth, and take a headshot with the camera or choose one from Photos.
5. Private studio notes stay in Kallos One and are never written to Contacts. Below them, the Timeline lists every shoot, signed release and statement, and Totals shows shoots, releases signed, TFP shoots, what you've paid them and what's still to pay. Tap Timeline & Totals near the top of the page to jump straight there.

**Tips**

- The Portfolio card on a model's page keeps their Instagram, portfolio link and agency, with buttons to open them, and Portfolio Photos for your best photos of them.
- If a model asks what you hold about them, open them and choose ⋯ › Export Their Data. You get one zip with their details, shoots, signed releases, statements, headshot and portfolio photos to send them. Signed releases may need to be kept as proof of consent.
- Every model has a Model card on their page: height and measurements (in cm or inches), dress and shoe size, hair and eye colour, tattoos and piercings, experience, the shoots they're interested in, availability, travel, TFP or paid, and how they heard about you. Tap Edit to change it. Adults-only interests appear only for models whose date of birth shows 18 or older.
- A model's date of birth goes into the Birthday field in Contacts when you save them there, and if you change the birthday in Contacts, Kallos One offers the update for you to review one person at a time (Update All never changes a birthday). If the new birthday changes whether they're under 18, or fills in one Kallos One didn't have, a warning asks you to check their ID before you accept. Kallos One writes a birthday to Contacts only when the contact has none, so a different birthday already there (such as a parent's) is never replaced. A birthday saved in Contacts without a year is ignored, as it can't show an age.
- Models can also send you their details through the model form on your website. The email you get has an Add to Kallos One button: tap it on your iPhone, iPad or Mac and Kallos One opens with everything filled in for you to check, including their Model card. Adults-only interests come through only for models 18 or older. Comfort level and private images are never set from the form by themselves: for a new model 18 or older, you confirm them with the model in the review (private images stay off unless you turn them on), and Fill In Missing Details never changes an existing model's comfort level or private images. Nothing is saved until you tap Save Model. If they're already in Kallos One (same email or phone), Fill In Missing Details adds only what's empty on their record.
- Add a nickname under the name if they go by one. It shows in brackets after their name, and syncs both ways with the Nickname field in Contacts.
- Importing someone who's already in Kallos One (same contact, phone number or email) updates them instead of adding them twice. Saving to Contacts updates their existing contact the same way, so you don't get duplicates.
- Phone numbers keep exactly the spacing and format they have in Contacts. If you only change the spacing in Contacts, Kallos One quietly follows it.
- A date of birth is required to sign a release, so the app can tell whether the model is under 18.
- On the model's page, Comfort level records how far they've told you they're happy to go: Clothed only, Swim / lingerie, Implied nude, Art nude — topless, or Art nude — full, plus whether they're OK with private (boudoir) images. It's for your records only. It can be set above Clothed only once their date of birth shows they're 18 or older, anything above Clothed shows a red 18+ badge, and every shoot still needs its own signed release.
- A "Needs Info" badge on a model card means a mandatory field is still missing: birthdate, a suburb or city, or a phone number or email. Open the model and the top of the page tells you exactly what to ask them to bring next time you meet. Comfort level, private notes and the headshot are optional and never trigger the badge.
- At the top of Models, the filter button narrows the list: Needs Info shows models still missing details, Comfort level shows models happy to go at least as far as you pick (Swim / Lingerie+, Implied+, Topless+ or Full nude), and Private images OK shows those fine with private images. Tap Clear Filters to see everyone again.
- Swipe a model left (or right-click it on a Mac) to delete them. Their contact stays in Contacts. To remove the contact too, open the model and choose ⋯ › Delete from Kallos One and Contacts.

### Model sign-up form for your website

Put a model sign-up form on your WordPress website. Each form is emailed to you with an Add to Kallos One button that fills in the model for you. Nothing is saved on your website.

1. Download the plugin: [https://ajunaidm.github.io/kallos-one-site/kallos-model-form.zip](https://ajunaidm.github.io/kallos-one-site/kallos-model-form.zip). On a Mac it lands in Downloads as kallos-model-form.zip. Keep it zipped.
2. In your WordPress dashboard (yoursite.com/wp-admin), go to Plugins › Add New › Upload Plugin, choose kallos-model-form.zip, tap Install Now, then Activate.
3. Go to Settings › Kallos Model Form. Check the email address forms go to and your studio name. Choose your country, cm or inches first, and your size system (AU, UK, US or EU). Tick the sections you want the form to show and which ones are required, then tap Save Changes.
4. Make a page for the form, for example Model Sign-Up, and give it a short web address such as yoursite.com/models.
5. Add the form to the page. Standard WordPress editor: add the Kallos Model Form block. WPBakery: add a Text Block, open its Text tab and type [kallos_model_form]. Elementor: add a Shortcode widget with [kallos_model_form]. Divi: a Text or Code module with [kallos_model_form]. Classic editor: paste [kallos_model_form] into the page. Then publish or update the page.
6. Send yourself a test: fill in the form on your website and tap Send my details.
7. Open the email on your iPhone, iPad or Mac and tap Add to Kallos One. Kallos One opens with the model and their Model card filled in. Check it and tap Save Model.

**Tips**

- Nothing is stored on your website: the details exist only in the email you receive. Replying to the email goes straight to the model.
- Under 18: a parent or guardian must add their details and agree before the form sends. Comfort level and adults-only shoot types are never shown to, or accepted from, anyone under 18.
- Form emails in your Junk folder? Mark one as Not Junk and add the sender to Contacts. For a lasting fix, install the free FluentSMTP plugin so your website sends email through your real email account.
- To update the plugin, download the new zip from the same link, upload it the same way and choose Replace current with uploaded. Your settings are kept.
- The plugin's settings also let you change the heading and the privacy line above the form, the consent sentence, a link to your privacy page, the thank-you message, the button text and colour, which details are required, and whether addresses are suggested while typing.
- The send button works only once everything marked * is filled in: the full address, Instagram or a portfolio link, experience, kind of work and at least one kind of shoot are needed unless you turn them off. While the model types the street, the form suggests addresses and fills in the rest: from Apple Maps (the same addresses as the app) once you add an Apple Maps token, otherwise from OpenStreetMap. The form tells them where what they type in that box goes.
- For the Apple Maps addresses, make a free token: in your Apple Developer account open Certificates, Identifiers & Profiles › Services › Maps › Configure › Tokens and tap +. Choose MapKit JS and Restriction Domain, enter your website address both with and without www, separated by a comma (for example ahmadjunaid.com, www.ahmadjunaid.com), choose No Expiration and tap Create. Copy the token and paste it in your WordPress Settings › Kallos Model Form › Apple Maps token.
- Add a sentence to your website's privacy page, for example: Details sent through the model form are emailed to us to arrange shoots and aren't stored on this website.
- If the form looks odd on your website (a pale button or faint tick boxes), clear your site's cache first. If it still looks wrong, contact support from Settings › Privacy & Data.

### Models you've worked with

A lifetime record of every model you've booked, with their details and every shoot, kept even after you delete them or archive their shoots.

1. Open Models. The Models You've Worked With card at the top shows your latest few; tap See All for everyone, with a search box.
2. Tap a name to see their details (email, phone, where they live, date of birth and age), their comfort level, totals (shoots booked and done, TFP and paid, agreed fees, first and latest shoot) and every shoot with its date, shoot type, place, status and whether the release was signed.
3. If they're still in your models, tap Open Model Page to go to their full page.

**Tips**

- The record is kept when you delete the model, delete a booking or archive old shoots, so the history is always there. It's text only, so it takes very little space.
- To remove someone's history, open their record and tap Delete This Record.
- Shoot types are recorded for bookings made from now on.
- The record also keeps the model's comfort level and whether they're OK with private images, as set on their page. It's for your records only: it never allows or skips anything, and every adults-only shoot still needs everyone's age checked.

### Add a client

Keep the brands, agencies and people who book you, with their bookings, contracts and invoices in one place.

1. Open Clients and tap +, then choose New Client or Import from Contacts. You can tick several contacts to import them at once.
2. Enter the contact's first, middle (optional) and last name and, if it's a business, the company name. The company is shown first.
3. On the client's page, add their address and tax ID if you invoice them, and any private notes.
4. Use Call, Message or Email at the top of the page to get in touch.
5. The Timeline lists every shoot, signed agreement and invoice for that client, newest first, and Totals shows shoots, agreements, what they've paid and what they still owe. Tap Timeline & Totals near the top of the page to jump straight there.

**Tips**

- Private notes stay in Kallos One and never appear on documents or in Contacts.
- Tap Add to Contacts to save a client to Contacts (name, company, email, phone and address), or Update from Contacts to copy their latest details.
- Swipe a client left (or right-click it on a Mac) to delete them. Their bookings, contracts and invoices are kept.

### Shoot types

Start any booking from one of 35 ready-made shoot types, from TFP and agency digitals to campaigns, boudoir and workshops, or save your own.

1. When you book, tap Type at the top of Book a Shoot and type to search, for example “look” for Fashion Lookbook. Your own types are listed first, then the suggestions by kind of work. Everything is filled in; change anything for this shoot.
2. To change a type, open Settings › Shoot Types and tap it. Set the length, whether there are models (TFP or their usual rate), a client, makeup and styling roles, the templates and the reminder.
3. To add one before you need it, tap New shoot type there and search the suggestions, or start from Blank.
4. Or set up a booking the way you like and tap Save as Shoot Type… at the bottom of the form.

**Tips**

- Each shoot type has a prep checklist (one item per line). The ready-made types come with their own four-line starting list — for example, a closed set and usage in writing for swimwear, or a property release for stock — and you can change it in the shoot type. When you book with a reminder, the checklist is added to it in Apple Reminders as tick boxes, and the widget's checklist shows that reminder so you can tick it off.
- A model or crew member you've booked before keeps the terms you agreed with them last time, even when a shoot type says otherwise.
- Shoot types that need extra care come with extra terms, such as Commercial use for campaigns, Stock licensing, Swimwear & lingerie, Nudity (adults only), Safety for water, fire or animals, Workshop, and Private images for boudoir. Your templates stay the base: each extra term is added to the release (or, for private images, the client agreement) only if your template doesn't already cover it. See or change them under Extra terms in the booking's Paperwork, or in the shoot type. A suggestion becomes one of your shoot types the first time you use it, so you can adjust its length, crew or notes for next time. Editing or deleting a shoot type never changes bookings already made with it.
- Swimwear / Lingerie, Boudoir, Glamour / Pin-up and Implied Nude / Art Nude are adults-only shoot types. See “Models under 18” for how Kallos One keeps them strictly 18+.
- When a model in the booking will be under 18 on the shoot date, adults-only shoot types and suggestions don't appear in the Type list at all, and a note under Type says why. Everywhere else, adults-only shoot types carry a small red 18+ badge so you can see at a glance they're never for anyone under 18.

### Shoot day and reminders

On the day, Bookings opens with everything for the shoot in one card, and Kallos One reminds you before and after.

1. On a shoot day (or within 12 hours of a shoot), the Today card at the top of Bookings shows the time, the location and everyone on the shoot.
2. Tap the location to open it in Maps. Tap a model or crew member to call or message them.
3. The card shows how many releases are signed. Tap Sign Releases to open the shoot and sign.
4. When the shoot ends, the button changes to Wrap Up & Invoice.
5. Kallos One reminds you an hour before a shoot if a release isn't signed, 30 minutes after it ends to wrap up, and the day after an invoice is due. Tap one to open it. Your usual heads-up is the alert you choose when booking, in Reminders.

**Tips**

- Allow notifications when Kallos One asks. To change them later, open Settings › Notifications › Kallos One (System Settings on a Mac). If they're off, a note at the top of Bookings reminds you.
- Reminders update by themselves when you move, cancel or wrap up a shoot, or mark an invoice paid.

### Weather on your shoots

Every booking card shows the forecast for the shoot day in °C and °F, with rain, cold, heat and wind tips added to your Apple Reminders note so you pack the right gear.

1. Add a location to the booking so Kallos One can look up its map pin.
2. The forecast is checked when you open the app and whenever you save a shoot. On iPhone and iPad it's also refreshed in the background a few times a day, so widgets and the packing reminder stay current even when Kallos One is closed. On the booking card, you'll see a condition icon and the day's expected temperature in both °C and °F.
3. Open the shoot's page to see the full card: high and low in both scales, rain chance, wind and UV index.
4. When the forecast suggests rain, cold, heat or high UV, or strong wind, suitable gear is added to your Apple Reminders note under "Weather tips" (rain cover, hand warmers, ND filters, sandbags and so on). Your own prep checklist is never changed.
5. You'll also get a packing reminder the day before, with a one-line summary of the forecast.

**Tips**

- Choose °C first or °F first in Settings › Weather on bookings. Everything else uses that order, including widgets and notifications.
- When rain or strong wind is likely for a shoot, the weather card offers Find a Better Day: the next 10 days at that place, marked Good or Poor. Pick one and the Reschedule screen opens at the same time on that day, so you can check clashes and tell everyone.
- Forecasts come from Apple WeatherKit using the booking's own location — Kallos One never asks for your current location.
- No forecast on the card? Check that the booking has a location, that it's within the next 10 days, and that you have an internet connection the next time you open Kallos One. If forecasts only update when you open the app, turn on Background App Refresh for Kallos One in the iPhone or iPad Settings app (General › Background App Refresh).

### Golden hour on your shoots

Every booking with a location shows sunrise, sunset and the golden hour for the shoot day, so you can plan outdoor light without doing the math.

1. Add a location to the booking so Kallos One knows where to look up the sun.
2. On the booking card and inside the shoot, you'll see one line under the weather: *Golden hour 4:48pm → 5:38pm · Sunset 5:38pm*. Earlier in the day it shows the morning window instead.
3. The same line is added to your Apple Reminders note, so the packing reminder the night before carries the light times too.
4. The call sheet PDF prints the full set — sunrise, golden hour and sunset — for everyone on the shoot.

**Tips**

- Nothing is ever sent anywhere: the times are worked out on the device from the booking's own map pin.
- Kallos One doesn't ask for your current location — the shoot's location is all it needs.
- At very high latitudes near midsummer or midwinter the sun may not cross the golden-hour angle; the line is hidden on those days.

### Share a call sheet

One-page PDF with everyone turning up, their phone numbers, the call time, the location, prep checklist, weather and light times. Share it the night before so nobody has to ask.

1. Open the shoot. Tap the ••• button in the top right and choose Share Call Sheet.
2. Kallos One builds a one-page A4 PDF from what it already knows about the booking — your studio details at the top, call time and location, models, crew, the client, your prep checklist, the forecast and the sunrise/sunset/golden-hour line.
3. Pick where to send it from the share sheet: AirDrop, Messages, Mail, or save to Files.

**Tips**

- Numbers come from each person's saved record, so make sure phones are set on your models and crew before you share.
- The prep checklist comes from the shoot type — edit it in Settings › Shoot Types if a line isn't quite right.
- Nothing new leaves Kallos One: no account, no cloud call, just a PDF the share sheet hands off.

### Plan a wedding or event

Plan weddings and events of any kind, over one day or many: Pakistani, Indian and other weddings, engagements, birthdays, family milestones, festivals, corporate and community events. Each day is its own booking, with its own weather, golden hour, calendar entry and reminder.

1. In Bookings, tap + and choose Plan an Event.
2. Choose what kind of event it is. Type to search over 120 kinds, from a Pakistani wedding (Dholki, Mayun, Mehndi, Nikah, Barat and Walima) to a first birthday, an Aqiqah, Diwali, an awards night or a fashion show.
3. Add who it's for. For a couple, choose Bride and Groom, Partner 1 and Partner 2, or your own words. Each name has an Urdu field beside it, written right to left. Everything here is optional while planning; English names are needed to sign the agreement, and Urdu names too for an Urdu agreement.
4. Check the suggested days. Rename, move or remove any day, untick the ones you aren't covering, or tap Add a Day. Moving the first day moves every day with it.
5. Add each day's venue and its theme or dress code, and write down the client's wishes. Under Money and agreement you can set the currency, pricing (whole package, per day, or package plus extra days), deposit, balance due date, overtime rate, photo count, which Event Agreement to use, and the Acting business (the trading name, logo, legal name and Tax ID that print on the agreement and invoices). Then tap Create Event.
6. Each day now shows in Bookings and Calendar with a pink line such as "Barat · Sara & Ali · Day 5 of 6". Use All, Shoots or Events at the top of Bookings to show just one kind.
7. To see the whole event, open any of its days and tap Open Event. There you can change names, contacts and wishes, add a day or delete the event.
8. Touch and hold (or right-click) a day in Bookings to choose Open Event straight away.

**Tips**

- The kind of event only suggests days and times. Nothing is fixed: change whatever suits the family.
- The event page brings it all together: People, Sensitivities, Shots and groups, Reference photos, Money, Deliverables, the Event Agreement, Countdown and After the event. Each day has its own Timeline and Run Sheet.
- The client's wishes stay in Kallos One and your own iCloud. They're never added to Calendar, notifications or Spotlight, and go to Apple Reminders only if you choose a Prepare reminder.
- Deleting an event also removes its timeline, people, lists, reference photos and countdown reminders. Any day with a signed contract, an invoice or a quote stays as an ordinary booking, a signed Event Agreement stays in Contracts, and you can undo for a few seconds.
- Children at events are normal and events are never adults-only. Words kept for adults-only shoots can't be used in an event's name, day names or themes, except “intimate” (as in “Intimate Nikah”); a bridal boudoir session is booked as its own shoot.
- Each day has a Venue & Rain button on its event card: write venue notes (parking, access, venue rules, the venue contact) and a rain plan. Both print on that day's call sheet and show on the run sheet, and if the forecast says rain is likely with no plan yet, the day reminds you.
- Renaming the couple or the event renames its days in Bookings and Calendar. Days you retitled yourself keep your title.
- On a Mac or an iPad with a keyboard, choose File › Plan an Event (⌥⌘N).

### Client's wishes

Write down what matters most to the client on any booking, a shoot or an event day, and get a reminder to prepare with their wishes as tick boxes.

1. Open the booking and find Client's wishes. Tap Add (or Edit).
2. Write their wishes in their words. Start a line with - to make it a tick box, for example - Candid photos of Nani.
3. Choose a Prepare reminder: 1 week, 3 days or 1 day before. It goes to Apple Reminders with the wishes as tick boxes.
4. Turn on Show on the call sheet only if your team should see them. It's off by default because wishes can be personal.
5. For lasting preferences, tap Save to the client's notes so they're there next time you book them.
6. Tap Save.

**Tips**

- Change the wishes or the Prepare choice at any time; the reminder is replaced, never duplicated.
- Wishes stay in Kallos One and your own iCloud. They're never added to Calendar, Spotlight or notifications; only a Prepare reminder you choose puts them in Apple Reminders.

### Plan a day's timeline

Every event day can have a timeline of moments (Baraat arrives, Nikah, stage photos, Rukhsati, first dance) with times, places and who's needed. Kallos One warns you when the drive between places doesn't fit.

1. Open an event day and tap Timeline on the event card.
2. Tap Add Suggested Moments for the usual moments of that day, or Add a Moment for your own. New events start with suggestions unless you turned that off.
3. Tap a moment to set its time, length, place (empty means the day's venue), who's needed and notes.
4. Turn on Fixed time for prayer times, ceremonies or venue rules. Turn on At golden hour to start it when the light is best at that place. Turn on Private for moments that mustn't be photographed.
5. When a moment is somewhere else, Kallos One checks the drive with Apple Maps and shows a warning with a Leave by time if the gap is too short.
6. On the day, swipe right to tick moments off (or touch and hold a moment). Tap Running Late to move everything still to come by 10 to 60 minutes; fixed moments stay put.
7. Tap Copy Another Day's Timeline to start from a similar day of the same event.

**Tips**

- The timeline prints on the day's call sheet, with fixed times and NO PHOTOS moments marked.
- Moments at another place get their own weather and golden hour. Forecasts reach about 10 days ahead, so further out a moment says when its weather will show.
- If the timeline runs past the end of coverage, the timeline tells you how long, as that time counts as overtime.

### Invoices and statements

Invoice clients and give models, makeup artists and stylists statements (including $0 TFP), standalone or from a booking. Save now and send whenever you're ready.

1. Open the Invoices tab. Filter by All, To Clients, To Models, To Crew or Unpaid; the top shows what clients owe you and what you've paid out. Tap it for this quarter's or financial year's totals and the GST figures for your BAS, then tap Export for Accountant (CSV) to send them a spreadsheet.
2. Tap + › New Invoice to Client, New Statement to Model or New Statement to Crew for a standalone one, or start from a booking: Invoice Client, or the Statement button for a model or crew member.
3. Check the recipient, shoot and business. Edit the lines (description, quantity and rate), add tax such as GST, set the due date and payment method, or turn on TFP for a $0 trade.
4. Tap Save PDF. It's saved as not sent yet, so you can preview it and send it now or later.
5. Open an invoice to preview the PDF, see its lines, mark it paid, or link it to a shoot. Tap Send to email it; after that the button says Resend. You can also swipe the invoice right and tap Send (on a Mac, right-click it).

**Tips**

- When you save an invoice or statement, Kallos One asks whether it's already been paid (you can change it later). TFP statements are never asked: they're labelled TFP, recorded as paid at $0, and left out of Money Summary, the BAS figures and the CSV export.
- Invoices from a booking start with the agreed client fee and rights, plus any paid models as reimbursement lines. Remove what doesn't apply.
- Statements to models and crew start from what you agreed: TFP, or their rate. Each person on a shoot gets their own.
- Unpaid client invoices past their due date show as Overdue. The money summary and accountant spreadsheet add up what you entered, by issue date; they're not tax advice, so check them with your accountant before lodging your BAS.
- Your ABN or Tax ID appears on the PDF only if it's filled in for the business.
- The list shows Not sent yet or the date it was sent. Emailing it marks it as sent; if you sent it another way, open it and tap Mark as Sent.

### Quotes, expenses and getting paid

Send quotes that become invoices, show clients how to pay (PayID, bank transfer, a Stripe or Square link with a QR code), take Tap to Pay with Square, and keep your expenses and their GST.

1. Set up how you get paid once: Settings › Studio & Brand Identity › your business › Getting paid. Add your PayID, bank details and a pay-online link made in your Stripe or Square account. They print on every unpaid invoice and quote, with a QR code for the link.
2. Quotes: tap + › New Quote in Invoices, New Quote on a client's page, or Make a Quote under Money on an event. Set the lines and the Valid until date and tap Save Quote, then share or email it. When the client accepts, open it in Invoices › Quotes and tap Convert to Invoice.
3. Your rate card: add your standard prices in Settings › Rate Card. In any quote or invoice, tap Add from Rate Card to put one on in a tap. Share Rate Card PDF sends the whole list.
4. Paid: open an invoice and turn on Paid by client. The date it was paid is recorded and can be changed under Paid on. Event pages and client statements show it.
5. Expenses: tap + › New Expense in Invoices (or open Expenses). Enter who you paid, the amount and the category, add a photo of the receipt, and link it to a shoot if you like. The GST in it is worked out as 1/11, and you can change it.
6. Client statement: on a client's page, tap Client Statement and choose a period. The PDF lists every invoice, what's paid and what's still owed, with how to pay.

**Tips**

- Quotes are never counted as money owed or earned. Only the invoice is.
- Expenses count in Money Summary: their GST is added to your GST credits (BAS 1B), they're in the accountant spreadsheet, and Left after fees and expenses takes them off. With more than one tax entity, each expense counts for its business's entity.
- Charge in Square (iPhone and iPad): install Square Point of Sale from the App Store and sign in with your own Square account; there's nothing to set up in Kallos One. On an unpaid client invoice, tap Charge in Square: Square opens with the amount filled in, so you can take Tap to Pay, a card or cash. When Square says it's paid, the invoice is marked paid. The money goes to the Square account signed in on that device. Charge in the currency of your Square account (AUD for an Australian account).
- Each invoice can have its own pay-online link (for example a Stripe or Square link for that exact amount); leave it blank to use your business's link.

### Shoot care: boundaries, closed set and the right to stop

Agree everything in writing before a sensitive shoot, so there are no surprises. Always on for adults-only shoots; turn it on for any other model shoot.

1. Open the booking. Adults-only shoots show the Shoot care card; on any other model shoot, tap Turn On on the Shoot care card.
2. Tap Agree (or Edit). Choose the level agreed, write what's planned and what's not included, and choose how touch is handled: ask first every time, or no touching.
3. Under On set, turn on Closed set and add anyone else who'll be there, and the model's support person if they're bringing one. Add a stop word if you like.
4. Turn on anything else you've agreed, such as a private place to change or no phones on set, and write the model's own wishes in their words.
5. Tap What to Expect Message to send the model the plan before the day. Read it, change anything, then send it by Messages or email.
6. When the model signs the release, they read Agreed for this shoot first, and it prints at the end of the release with the right to stop.

**Tips**

- Choosing a level above Clothed makes the shoot adults-only: everyone must be 18 or older, with no exceptions.
- If the level is above a model's comfort level on their model page, an orange note reminds you to check with them.
- The Right to stop clause is added to every adults-only release and every shoot with shoot care. Turn on Image review before publishing in Extra terms if you'll show the model the selects first.
- The call sheet shows the closed set, the support person and the stop word, never the boundaries. On the day, the Today card reminds you to check in before each new look.
- Add an Intimacy Coordinator or Chaperone to the crew for shoots where you book one.
- Settings › How I Work holds your code of conduct. Tap Use the Ready-Made Version, change it to your own words, then Share as PDF with models before they book, or Copy Text for your website.
- Shoot care stays on your device and your iCloud: never in Calendar, notifications, widgets or Spotlight.
- Turn on Write in Urdu in the What to Expect message or the How I Work email to write it in Urdu. Settings › How I Work has an English and an اردو version; the Urdu one is attached when you write in Urdu.
- After you book any model shoot, Kallos One offers a How I Work email for the model: a short letter about how you work and that they can stop at any time, your How I Work PDF and the shoot's details (and what you agreed, if you like). Read it, change anything, and tap Open in Mail. Send it later with How I Work Email on the Shoot care card.

### Sign a model release

Get the release signed before the shoot. Kallos One creates a sealed A4 PDF.

5. The signer reads the electronic signature consent, turns on I consent to sign electronically, then taps the signature pad and signs full screen.
6. Tap Sign Model Release. The PDF appears with Share, Email, JPEG and Verify.

**Tips**

- Check before signing never shows your private notes or the model's comfort level.
- On the signing pad, the iPhone turns sideways to give the whole screen to the signature. Tap Done to keep it, or tap the signature later to sign again.

### Sign an Event Agreement

One agreement for a whole wedding or event, in English, Urdu, or both. The Schedule of days, fees and deliverables is filled in from the event, and the deposit and balance invoices come from the same numbers.

1. Open the event (open any of its days and tap Open Event). Under Money, set the currency, the pricing (whole package, per day, or package plus extra days), the deposit, the balance due date and the overtime rate.
2. Under Deliverables, enter about how many edited photos, the sneak peek (how many, and within how many days), and any album, prints or video. Check the gallery due date (30 days after the last day unless you choose one) and choose whether you may use the photos yourself: Yes, Ask first or No. Under On the agreement you'll see the line that prints, in Urdu too for Urdu agreements; type your own words to replace it.
3. Under Event Agreement, choose the agreement: Event Agreement (Australia), or for Pakistan the Urdu, English, or Urdu and English version.
4. Tap Sign Event Agreement. Read the agreement with the Schedule filled in: every day with its times and venue, the team, deliverables, fees and payments. If a name is missing (in English, or in Urdu for an Urdu agreement), Kallos One says which one to add on the event page first.
5. Hand the device to the client. In Urdu agreements the consent, the switch and the signature box are in Urdu for them. They consent to sign electronically and sign.
6. Tap Sign Event Agreement. The sealed PDF appears with Share, Email, JPEG and Verify, and is kept in Contracts with an Event label.
7. Back on the event, tap Invoice Deposit or Invoice Balance to make the invoice with the amounts and currency already filled in.

**Tips**

- These are starting templates. Have your lawyer check them, and edit any wording, including the numbers in [brackets], in Settings › Contract Templates.
- Urdu prints right to left in a Nastaliq font, on screen and in the PDF. In the Urdu and English version, Urdu comes first and prevails if the two differ.
- Extra terms (such as Adults only or Private images) are added in Urdu to Urdu templates, and in both languages to Urdu and English templates.
- An Urdu agreement prints entirely in Urdu: the agreement, the Schedule, the client details and the signature labels. Urdu and English agreements print both. Only the technical audit line at the very bottom stays in English.
- Wishes, notes and family details never print on the agreement.
- Anything still blank (such as the deposit or deliverables) is listed before signing so you can fill it in first.

### Models under 18

Kallos One never allows a shoot involving nudity or implied nudity, lingerie, underwear, swimwear, boudoir, glamour or anything sexualised for anyone under 18. No exceptions, even with a parent or guardian's consent.

1. Models under 18 can only be booked for appropriate shoots, such as portfolio, fashion, family and portrait work. A parent or guardian signs the release and must be present.
2. A shoot is adults-only when it has an adults-only extra term (Adults only, Nudity, Swimwear & lingerie or Private images), or when its title, description, shoot type or release template mentions such content.
   If anyone in the booking will be under 18 on the shoot date, adults-only shoot types aren't offered in the Type list at all.
3. For an adults-only shoot, Book stays greyed out until every model's date of birth is filled in, everyone is 18 or older on the shoot date, and you confirm that everyone photographed is 18 or older and you've checked ID.
4. If a model under 18 is added later, or a date of birth changes, the booking shows a red stop, the people can't be saved, and the release can't be signed. The client agreement asks the signer to confirm everyone is 18 or older.
5. Adults-only contracts include an Adults only clause, confirming everyone photographed is 18 or older and that no one under 18 takes part or is present.

**Tips**

- In an adults-only booking, a gentle note lists any model whose comfort level is below what the shoot needs (for example, set to Swim / Lingerie on a shoot that needs Topless), or who isn't marked OK with private images on a private shoot, so you can check with them. It's a reminder, not a block.
- Turning off an adults-only extra term asks you to confirm the shoot has none of that content.
- A model's comfort level is for your records only. It stays at Clothed only for anyone under 18 or without a date of birth, and it never overrides the age checks.
- If you suspect a child is being exploited, call the police on 000 in an emergency, or report it at accce.gov.au/report.

### Hand over with Signing mode

Kallos One locks itself to the contract while it's being signed, so the signer can't see anything else.

1. Open the model release or client agreement. Signing mode turns on by itself: the tabs, the list and the Back button are put away, and a Signing mode bar appears at the bottom.
2. Fill in anything missing, then hand over the device.
3. The signer reads, consents and signs as usual.
4. When you get it back, tap Exit in the Signing mode bar and confirm with Face ID, Touch ID or your passcode (on a Mac, Touch ID or your Mac password).

**Tips**

- Signing mode stays on after signing, so the signer can't look around once they're done. Once the signer consents or signs, the details on the contract lock; to fix one, tap Edit Details and confirm with Face ID, Touch ID or your passcode. The signature is cleared, so it's signed again on the corrected details. To turn it back on after exiting, tap the lock button at the top.
- If the device has no passcode, anyone can exit. Set a passcode to keep Signing mode locked.
- Model releases and English client agreements are signed on an English, left-to-right screen even when Kallos One is in Urdu, so the signer reads the same English consent the PDF records. Agreements with Urdu wording keep your language.

### Sign full screen

Every signature is drawn on a large pad, so signers have room to sign naturally.

1. Tap any signature pad (or ⋯ › Sign).
2. The iPhone turns sideways and the whole screen becomes the signing pad.
3. The signer signs above the line, then taps Done. Use Clear to start again or Cancel to go back.
4. The finished signature appears on the page. Tap it to sign again, or tap Clear to remove it.

**Tips**

- On iPad and Mac the pad fills the screen or window without rotating.

### Use a photo of a signature

Turn a photo of a signature on paper into a clean signature. Best for your own master signature.

1. Sign on white paper in dark ink and take a clear, well-lit photo.
2. On the signature pad, tap ⋯ › Use Photo of Signature and choose the photo.
3. Kallos One removes the paper and keeps only the ink.

**Tips**

- For model releases, have the model sign live. A photo doesn't show they signed that document at that time.

## Privacy & troubleshooting

### Privacy, backups and deleting

Your data stays on your device, or in your own private iCloud when sync is on. Here's how to keep copies and delete things.

1. Kallos One collects no data and has no servers. Everything stays on your device, or in your own private iCloud when sync is on.
2. To keep copies of contracts, use Share › Save to Files, Settings › Privacy & Data › Export All Signed Contracts, or a full backup in Settings › Backup & Restore.
3. Delete one booking with everything linked to it: swipe it left in Bookings (on a Mac, right-click it). Expenses and quotes linked to it are kept, as your tax and business records.
4. Deleted something by mistake? Tap Undo in the bar at the bottom of the screen within a few seconds (on a Mac, click Undo or press ⌘Z).
5. Erase everything: Settings › Privacy & Data › Delete All Data, then type DELETE.

**Tips**

- Settings › Privacy & Data › Activity Log lists what happened to your paperwork and data: contracts signed, invoices saved and paid, quotes, bookings, events and templates deleted, backups, restores and archives, with the date and device. Tap Export for a spreadsheet.
- If your last full backup is more than a month old (two months with iCloud sync on), a Time to back up card appears in Bookings. Tap Back Up Now, or Remind Me Next Week.
- Once the Undo bar has gone, deleting can't be undone. Delete All Data and removing a contact from Contacts always ask first. Signed releases may be your proof of consent, so export them first.

### Sync across your devices

Keep Kallos One the same on your iPhone, iPad and Mac through your own private iCloud.

1. On each iPhone, iPad and Mac, sign in to the same Apple Account with iCloud turned on, and install Kallos One.
2. Sync with iCloud is on by default. To check or change it, open Settings › Cloud & Multi-Device Sync. A change here applies the next time you open Kallos One.
3. Bookings, models, clients, makeup artists and stylists, contracts, invoices, templates and business profiles then stay in step on their own. Signed PDFs are kept in your private iCloud too.

**Tips**

- The line under the switch shows whether sync is active or your data is on this device only.
- Each device keeps its own copy, so Kallos One works without internet. Changes upload when you're back online.
- Added the same person or shoot on two devices? Kallos One merges them: people with the same name and the same email, phone or birthday, and bookings with the same title and start time. Business profiles are merged only when the legal name, Tax ID and trading name all match, so trading names under one Tax ID stay separate.
- Appearance and the Face ID or Touch ID lock are set separately on each device.
- Deleting something on one device deletes it on all of them. Make a backup before using Delete All Data.

### Back up and restore

Save your setup, or everything, to a file, and bring it back after reinstalling or on another device.

1. Open Settings › Backup & Restore.
2. Choose Settings Only (business profiles, logos, master signature, templates, shoot types, governing law and preferences) or Everything (also models, clients, makeup artists and stylists, bookings, weddings and events, releases and invoices with their PDFs).
3. Turn on Protect with a password (recommended for Everything), then tap Create Backup… and choose where to save it: Files or iCloud Drive on iPhone and iPad, or any folder or iCloud Drive on a Mac.
4. To restore, tap Choose Backup File…, pick the .kallosbackup file from Files, iCloud Drive or any folder on your Mac, enter its password if it has one, check the summary and tap Restore.

**Tips**

- A settings backup keeps your models, clients, bookings, releases and invoices; a full backup replaces everything on this device.
- Back up before deleting the app, changing phones or using Delete All Data.
- Lost passwords can't be recovered. After restoring, turn the Face ID or Touch ID lock back on if you use it.

### Archive old shoots

Keep Kallos One light: move shoots from more than 3 months ago, with their contracts, invoices and PDFs, to a complete archive in a folder you choose.

1. Open Settings › Archive Old Shoots (in Sync & Backup), or tap Archive… on the “Time to archive old shoots” card in Bookings.
2. Tap Choose Folder… and pick where your archive lives: iCloud Drive, Files, an external drive, or any folder on a Mac. Kallos One creates a “Kallos One Archive” folder there.
3. Check the list of shoots ready to archive, then tap Archive … Shoots… and confirm. Kallos One adds them, with the models, clients and crew involved, their signed contracts and invoices and the PDFs, to “Kallos One Archive.kallosbackup”, checks it, and only then removes those shoots from Kallos One (and from your other devices when iCloud sync is on).
4. Shoots waiting for payment are listed separately with what's owed. Tap one and choose Mark as Paid if it's been settled (it can then be archived), or Keep for Settlement to leave it in Kallos One until it's paid.
5. With Also save PDFs as separate files on, every contract and invoice is also saved in Kallos One Archive › PDFs, by year and shoot, so you can open it any time without restoring. Copy All PDFs to the Folder Now saves every PDF still in the app without removing anything.
6. To bring archived shoots back, open Settings › Backup & Restore › Choose Backup File… and pick “Kallos One Archive.kallosbackup”. They're added back; nothing else is changed or replaced.

**Tips**

- Only shoots that finished (or were cancelled) more than 3 months ago are archived. Upcoming shoots, past shoots not yet wrapped up and shoots with an unpaid invoice always stay.
- Each time, new shoots are added to the same archive file, and the previous version is kept as “Kallos One Archive (previous copy)”. If anything can't be read or checked, nothing is removed.
- Models, clients and crew stay in Kallos One, and calendar events aren't changed. Kallos One reminds you every 3 months, only when there's something to archive.
- Turn on Protect the archive with a password to encrypt the archive file. The password is kept in your iCloud Keychain so your other devices can add to the same archive, and you'll need it to bring shoots back. It can't be recovered if you forget it. The separate PDF copies aren't covered, so turn off Also save PDFs as separate files if you want everything protected.

### Sample data and What's New

Try Kallos One with a sample studio, kept apart from your own work, and see what changed after each update.

1. To try Kallos One without touching your own work, open Settings › Appearance and turn on Use sample data.
2. Close Kallos One and open it again (on a Mac, quit and reopen it). Bookings shows a sample studio, with a banner along the top so you always know.
3. Try anything: book, plan an event, sign, invoice. It all stays in the sample data.
4. When you're done, turn off Use sample data and reopen Kallos One. Your own work comes back exactly as you left it.
5. After each update, Kallos One shows What's New once. To see it again, open Settings › Privacy & Data › What's New.

**Tips**

- Sample data is kept apart on this device. It never syncs to iCloud and never goes to Calendar, Reminders, notifications, Spotlight or Live Activities.
- Backups, Archive Old Shoots and Delete All Data are off while you're using sample data, so your real files and settings are never mixed with the samples.

### Troubleshooting

Quick fixes for the most common problems.

1. Shoots don't appear in Calendar: allow Kallos One full access in Settings › Privacy & Security › Calendars (System Settings on a Mac), and make sure iCloud Calendars is turned on (Kallos One doesn't use Google or Exchange calendars).
2. Contacts won't update: allow access in Settings › Privacy & Security › Contacts (System Settings on a Mac).
3. No signature found in a photo: use dark ink on plain white paper, in good light, filling most of the photo.
4. A backup won't open: check the password (capital letters matter). A backup made by a newer version of Kallos One needs the app updated first.
5. Locked out after Face ID or Touch ID fails: tap Unlock again and choose Enter Passcode (or your Mac password).

**Tips**

- Still stuck? Settings › Privacy & Data › Contact Support.
